About

One company, two missions.

SIVYIS began with a question: what is music worth to you? That became Cosmoly — an infinite 3D universe where every star is an artist, and fans can support them directly with Bitcoin.

The same thinking applies to energy. Large flexible loads such as Bitcoin miners are reshaping how grids are run, and public understanding lags behind. Loadline exists to explain it with sources, not slogans.

Founded by Txai Xiong. Both products are in active development.

  • Cosmoly — music discovery & artist support (public demo)
  • Loadline — grid & Bitcoin-mining education and transparency (draft)
  • Approach — show the receipts, label what is a demo, cite sources

Cosmoly

A galaxy-style music app. Fly through genre clusters, tap a star to open an artist profile, and explore music history and philosophy.

  • 3D galaxy of genres and artists
  • Artist profiles, store and merch links
  • Bitcoin wallet and tipping, as a demo
Honest status: a public demo. The wallet is simulated and stored on your device only; audio is generated in-browser and artists are sample content. Not a streaming service yet.

Loadline

A neutral map and education hub on Bitcoin mining and the power grid, built so utilities, miners and the public can argue from the same numbers.

  • Explainers with a sources list for every figure
