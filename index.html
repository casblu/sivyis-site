Vision

The shaman and the instrument.

We think of SIVYIS as a shaman playing the vibrations, energy and power cycles of the world like an instrument, casting a healing spell to heal the universe.

Cycles

Small to huge, one pattern.

Nested rhythms we can all recognize, from the body to the cosmos.

  1. HeartbeatAbout a secondA resting adult heart beats roughly 60–100 times a minute.
  2. BreathA few secondsA resting adult breathes about 12–20 times a minute.
  3. Day24 hoursEarth turns once on its axis, giving us day and night.
  4. TidesAbout 12.4 hoursOn many coasts the sea rises and falls twice a day, pulled by the Moon and Sun.
  5. Grid loadDaily & seasonalElectricity demand rises and falls with the day and the seasons, and grids hold a steady frequency: 60 Hz in North America, 50 Hz in much of the world.
  6. SeasonsOne yearEarth’s axial tilt, about 23.4°, and its orbit around the Sun bring the seasons.
  7. Solar cycleAbout 11 yearsSunspot activity swells and fades roughly every 11 years.
  8. EpochsThousands of yearsWe live in the Holocene, which began about 11,700 years ago. Earth itself is about 4.5 billion years old.
  9. Galactic year~225–250 million yearsAbout how long the Sun takes to orbit the center of the Milky Way.

Branch · Data Sonification

Hear the cycles in your data.

Every dataset has a rhythm: daily load, a heartbeat of a sensor, the weekly pulse of a business. Sonification maps values to sound so you can notice change by ear, when your eyes are busy, or when you cannot see a screen. It is the same mission as the other branches: attention to cycles, vibration and frequency, made practical.

  • Monitoring
  • Science
  • Accessibility
  • Education
  • Art & music

SIVYIS Sonify — free tier

A free web app for researchers, economists, quants and labs. Upload, paste or link a CSV or JSON file, map each column to pitch, volume, pan, timbre or rhythm, and follow the sound on a synced chart. Export WAV or MIDI and share a link. Examples use real public data with sources and dates: Texas grid load, Mauna Loa CO₂, the Treasury yield curve and CPI. It runs in your browser with no ads and no trackers, and your data is not uploaded.

Giving back

Bomb-free land in Laos.

SIVYIS Corp pledges 5% of profits from every product — Cosmoly, Loadline, SIVYIS Sonify, Haichoppler and every future brand — to be shared between Legacies of War and MAG (Mines Advisory Group).

Legacies of War is a Lao American and women-led movement advocating for families in Laos, Cambodia and Vietnam to live on bomb-free land, free of the unexploded ordnance (UXO) left by U.S. bombing during the Vietnam War era.

MAG (Mines Advisory Group) is a humanitarian organization that finds and destroys landmines and unexploded bombs in conflict-affected countries, including Laos.

Visit Legacies of War ↗Visit MAG ↗
  • 1964–1973: the U.S. dropped over two million tons of ordnance on Laos in 580,000 bombing sorties.
  • 270 million+ cluster munitions were dropped on Laos; about 30% failed to detonate.
  • Most bombed country per capita in history: Laos.
  • Only about 10% of UXO in Laos has been cleared.

Source: legaciesofwar.org, accessed Oct 19, 2026; maginternational.org, accessed Oct 20, 2026. The pledge applies to profits as they are earned; SIVYIS Corp is not affiliated with Legacies of War or MAG.
